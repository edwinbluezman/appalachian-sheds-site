Built With Purpose

Superior Standard Features

Quality materials and thoughtful construction practices are foundational to every Appalachian Sheds building. Final structural details are tailored to building configuration, site conditions, and approved plans.

▦Foundation & Support

Pressure-treated 4×6 beams on 16×16×4-inch solid concrete pads where applicable. Engineered support layout and anchorage follow the building's configuration and site requirements.

▤Solid Floor Construction

2×6 pressure-treated floor joists at 16 inches on center and ¾-inch pressure-treated exterior-rated plywood floor decking where included in the selected design.

▥Quality Wall Framing

2×4 kiln-dried framing studs at 16 inches on center, with framing and attachment details appropriate to each configuration.

⌂Single-Slope Roofline

Distinctive 2/12 mono-slope roof with engineered roof structure and ½-inch CDX roof sheathing. Roofing and low-slope water protection specified to suit the approved assembly.

◇Exterior Character

Choice of five siding options, distinctive cedar trim and Craftsman-style wood doors; final openings and details are identified in your proposal.

✓Built on Your Property

All Laurel Ridge buildings are constructed on site. Site access, required approvals, support elevations and property conditions are reviewed in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the Attached Lean-To and Stand-Alone Laurel Ridge?

The Attached Lean-To is planned alongside an existing building and requires assessment of the attachment and water-management details. The Stand-Alone Laurel Ridge is a separate building with independent support and enclosure requirements. Both are constructed on site.

Why is Stand-Alone pricing provided by written proposal?

A separate foundation and enclosure, structural connections, roof details and site conditions influence the total. A written proposal ensures your pricing reflects the building actually planned for your property.

Are all 24 Attached Lean-To sizes available?

Yes, the published comparison covers all 24 listed sizes, including the 4-foot-wide options. We can also discuss other sizing needs for your property.

Does the price include a finished interior?

No. Standard pricing is for a weather-tight exterior shell. Interior finishes, electrical, insulation, climate control and other improvements are not included unless they are expressly included in your written proposal.

Do I need to paint or stain the exterior?

Required exterior caulking/sealing and painting or staining must be completed within 30 days of construction as applicable to the siding and trim selected. Appalachian Sheds offers an optional complete exterior painting service.

Are Laurel Ridge buildings constructed on site?

Every Laurel Ridge is constructed on site. Our team reviews access, conditions and placement before the work begins.

Will the guide price be my final contract price?

The guide helps you compare Attached Lean-To sizes and siding options. Your written proposal confirms the scope, site requirements, selected upgrades and final project price.
