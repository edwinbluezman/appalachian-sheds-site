Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between overall and enclosed size?

Overall dimensions include the building's recessed entry area. Enclosed dimensions identify the enclosed portion of the structure. Both are shown alongside each price.

How is my Nordic Retreat constructed?

Appalachian Sheds constructs every building directly on your property, with site-specific access and foundation requirements addressed in the written proposal.

Are interiors finished?

No. Standard pricing is for an exterior weather-tight shell only. Interior wall, ceiling, electrical and other finish work is outside the standard scope and may be discussed separately.

Does the quoted price include painting?

Exterior finishing is not included in standard shell pricing. Required caulking and painting or staining must be completed within 30 days. Optional finishing services are available separately.

How are site conditions handled?

The final written proposal addresses property access, foundation support, leveling, anchorage, site conditions, upgrades and any necessary additional preparation.
