A Letter From Our Founder · Cincinnati, Ohio

A Better Way to Build. A Tradition Worth Keeping.

There is something special about the Appalachian Mountains.

Perhaps it's the quiet strength of the mountains themselves. Perhaps it's the generations of hardworking families who built their homes, barns, workshops, and livelihoods with little more than honest materials, skilled hands, and an unwavering belief in doing things right.

Or perhaps it's something simpler.

The belief that a person's word should mean something, that good work deserves respect, and that anything worth building is worth building well.

Those are the values that inspired Appalachian Sheds Inc.

And they're the values I believe are worth carrying forward.

When Craftsmanship Was Personal

There was a time when a family needed a barn, a workshop, or a simple backyard building, they called upon someone from their own community.

A craftsman would come to their property, listen to what they needed, study the land, and build something that belonged there.

There were no assembly lines. No buildings designed primarily around the limitations of highway transportation. No expectation that every family should settle for something that looked just like everyone else's.

The work was personal.

The craftsman took pride in what he built. The homeowner knew who built it. And the finished building represented something more than lumber, nails, and a roof.

It represented a promise.

Over time, much of that tradition gave way to mass production. Factories brought speed, convenience, and affordability, and those advancements served an important purpose.

But somewhere along the way, something valuable began to disappear.

The connection between the person who needed something built and the person who took pride in building it.

I believe it's time to bring that connection back.

Bringing the Craftsman Back to the Backyard

At Appalachian Sheds Inc., we're not interested in becoming another mass-production shed company.

We're building something different.

We believe a backyard building should be more than a prefabricated box delivered on the back of a truck.

It should be thoughtfully designed, carefully constructed, and suited to the property and the people who will use it.

That's why our buildings are constructed right on your property by qualified craftsmen who understand that the details matter.

Building on site gives us the freedom to embrace the architectural character that makes Appalachian structures so distinctive: steep rooflines, inviting porches, traditional siding, generous proportions, and the kind of enduring design that never goes out of style.

It also gives homeowners an opportunity to create something genuinely their own.

Maybe it's a garden shed that reminds you of an old mountain cottage. A workshop where you'll spend your retirement years creating things with your hands. A backyard studio where a new idea becomes a small business. Or simply a beautiful place to store the tools and treasures of everyday life.

Whatever its purpose, your building should feel like it belongs to your home, your landscape, and your way of life.

Good Work. Good People. Something Worth Having.

I have always believed there is dignity in honest work.

There is pride in standing back at the end of the day, looking at something you've built, and knowing you gave it your best.

That sense of accomplishment matters.

It matters to the carpenter who measures twice because he cares about getting it right. It matters to the homeowner who appreciates a well-built structure. And it matters to the families and communities supported by skilled, meaningful work.

Our mission is built around one simple belief:

Homeowners deserve something worth having. Craftsmen deserve work worth doing.

When those two things come together, something good happens.

Homeowners receive buildings constructed with care and attention. Craftsmen have an opportunity to take ownership of their work and develop relationships with the people they serve. And our communities benefit when local skill, enterprise, and craftsmanship are valued.

That, to me, is a better way of doing business.

Inspired by the Mountains. Built for Generations.

The Appalachian Mountains have always represented something meaningful to me.

Not merely their beauty, but the character of the people and places that have endured there.

Self-reliance. Resourcefulness. Hospitality. Family. Faith in one's abilities. Pride in a job well done.

These values have been passed from one generation to the next, not through grand speeches, but through everyday actions.

A grandfather teaching his grandson how to use a framing square.

A neighbor helping raise a barn.

A craftsman signing his name to work he knows will stand long after he's gone.

We may use modern tools, improved materials, and better building methods today, but the principles behind good craftsmanship haven't changed.

Build with care. Treat people fairly. Keep your word. And leave something behind that makes you proud.

That is the spirit we hope you'll recognize in every Appalachian Sheds building.

An Invitation to Build Something Meaningful

When you choose Appalachian Sheds Inc., you're doing more than purchasing a backyard structure.

You're choosing thoughtful design over the ordinary.

You're choosing skilled craftsmanship over anonymous mass production.

You're supporting a veteran- and family-owned business that believes relationships are just as important as results.

And you're helping preserve a tradition that deserves a place in the future.

We're proud to serve homeowners throughout Greater Cincinnati and the surrounding Ohio, Kentucky, and Indiana communities.

Whether you're dreaming of a simple garden shed, a beautiful backyard workshop, or a distinctive Appalachian-inspired building designed around your life, we'd be honored to help bring that vision to life.

Because at the end of the day, our business isn't simply about building sheds.

It's about building something worth having, doing work worth doing, and keeping the best of an old tradition alive for generations to come.

From our family to yours, thank you for considering Appalachian Sheds Inc.

With appreciation,

Edwin R. Shackelford
Founder, Appalachian Sheds Inc.
