The construction behind the character

Quality begins long before the finishing touches.

A good-looking backyard structure deserves a strong foundation, consistent framing practices, and careful attention to the details that help protect the building.

01 / BELOW THE FLOOR

A substantial foundation

Pressure-treated 4×6 foundation beams are supported on 16×16×4-inch solid concrete pads, with support locations and anchorage determined for the building.

02 / UNDER YOUR FEET

A hardworking floor system

Pressure-treated 2×6 joists at 16 inches on center support ¾-inch exterior-rated, pressure-treated plywood floor decking.

03 / BEHIND THE SIDING

Reliable wall framing

2×4 kiln-dried wall studs at 16 inches on center form a carefully assembled framework for the building envelope.

04 / OVERHEAD

Purposeful roof structure

Traditional models use engineered 2×4 roof trusses at 24 inches on center, with roof framing adapted as needed to each roof style.

05 / ABOVE THE FRAMING

A protective roof assembly

½-inch CDX plywood sheathing, synthetic underlayment and architectural asphalt shingles form the specified standard roof assembly, subject to roof-specific requirements.

06 / AT EVERY OPENING

Attention to weather protection

Compatible window and door flashing, sill drainage details and perimeter sealing contribute to a weather-tight exterior shell.

Foundation & floor system

Built to support everyday use.

Our base floor system pairs pressure-treated structural members with a durable plywood floor deck.

  • 4×6 pressure-treated foundation beams
  • 16×16×4-inch solid concrete support pads
  • 2×6 pressure-treated floor joists at 16″ on center
  • ¾-inch pressure-treated, exterior-rated plywood decking
  • Pad spacing, beam support and anchorage are determined by building size, loading and project requirements

Standard leveling covers up to 6 inches of out-of-level conditions; additional site work is quoted separately in writing.

Wall construction

Consistent framing behind the siding.

Our traditional wall framing provides the structure for the building's exterior walls and installed openings.

  • 2×4 kiln-dried framing studs
  • 16-inch on-center stud spacing
  • Framed window and door openings
  • Model-specific wall geometry and connections
  • Exterior siding choices selected separately on each model pricing page

Interior wall finishes, insulation and electrical work are not included in the standard exterior-shell package.

Roof framing

Designed for each roofline.

Roof structure varies among traditional gables, mono-slope designs, dormers and gambrel forms.

  • Traditional models: engineered 2×4 trusses at 24″ on center
  • ½-inch CDX plywood roof sheathing
  • Architectural asphalt shingles with synthetic underlayment where permitted by the roof assembly requirements
  • Ridge and soffit ventilation on applicable assemblies
  • Specialty roof designs use model-specific framing and approved weather protection details

Low-slope and specialty roofs may require enhanced underlayment or different roof assembly details.

Exterior protection

Protection is in the details.

A weather-tight exterior shell depends on thoughtful material transitions, properly installed openings and timely exterior finishing.

  • Manufacturer-compatible window and door flashing
  • Appropriate sill drainage and perimeter sealing
  • Roseburg DuraTemp standard siding on traditional models
  • LP SmartSide and GP Vinyl options where offered
  • Cedar trim, fascia and model-specific details

Required exterior caulking, painting or staining must be completed within 30 days of construction, as applicable; ASI offers a separate painting service.

Know exactly what you are purchasing

An exterior weather-tight shell. No surprises about the interior.

Your standard Appalachian Sheds building is constructed on site with its exterior weather-tight building shell. Unless your written proposal expressly includes them, the interior framing remains exposed and interior finishing is not part of the standard price.

Exterior finishing is important.

Required exterior caulking and painting or staining must be completed within 30 days after construction, in accordance with the applicable siding and trim materials and their requirements.

Appalachian Sheds offers a complete exterior painting service as an optional addition to your written proposal.

Answers to common questions

What you'll want to know.

Will the interior walls and roof be finished?

No. The standard building is an exterior weather-tight shell with exposed interior framing. Interior finishing is not included unless specifically listed in your written proposal.

Are all Appalachian Sheds models framed exactly the same way?

No. Many models share standard floor and wall framing methods, but roof style, window and door layout, size and engineering requirements can change the structural design.

Do you build the building on my property?

Yes. All Appalachian Sheds buildings are Built on Site. Site conditions, leveling requirements and any additional preparation are reviewed as part of planning and the written proposal.

When does the exterior need to be painted or stained?

Required exterior caulking, painting or staining must be completed within 30 days after construction, depending on the selected siding and trim requirements. A complete exterior painting service is available as an optional addition.

Can I request additional interior improvements?

Ask about available project-specific options. Any agreed insulation, interior finishes or other improvements must be identified separately in the written proposal; they are not included in the standard building shell.

Are the diagrams exact building plans?

No. The diagrams are explanatory illustrations. Final framing, support, connections and roof details depend on the selected model, building size, local requirements and project specifications.
