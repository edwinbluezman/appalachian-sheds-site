What Sets Us Apart

Superior Standard Features

Solid Foundation System

Pressure-treated 4×6 support beams on 16×16×4-inch solid concrete pads; support spacing and anchorage designed for the building and site.

Substantial Floor Structure

2×6 pressure-treated floor joists at 16 inches on center with ¾-inch pressure-treated exterior-rated plywood decking.

Built-to-Last Wall Framing

2×4 kiln-dried studs at 16 inches on center.

Traditional 8/12 Roof

Engineered roof trusses at 24 inches on center, ½-inch CDX plywood sheathing and architectural asphalt shingles.

Roof Ventilation

Ridge vent ventilation, with manufacturer-compatible roof details.

Craftsman-Style Details

Natural cedar trim, two 24×30-inch single-hung windows and site-built Craftsman double doors with black hardware.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Is The Pine Ridge built on my property?

Yes. Every Appalachian Sheds building is constructed on site. Every building is constructed on site.

Is the interior finished at the listed price?

No. The standard price covers a weather-tight exterior shell, not a finished interior. Interior improvements can be discussed and quoted separately.

Do I need to paint or stain my Pine Ridge?

Required exterior caulking/sealing and painting or staining must be completed within 30 days after construction, as applicable to the chosen siding and trim. Our complete exterior painting service is available as an option.

Can I request a size that isn't featured above?

Yes. Our full price comparison includes 21 sizes, and we can discuss other dimensions and configuration needs for your property.

Will my final project price match the guide?

The guide helps you plan. Your written proposal reflects your selected building, actual site conditions, access, required approvals and any optional upgrades or services.
